# Assorted Ice Pixie

Adds ice pixies to snowy biomes. They throw ice at you and only fire can hurt them.

Also included in [Assorted Mobs](../mobs). Worlds made with Assorted Mobs 1.x keep everything when you switch to this mod.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib).

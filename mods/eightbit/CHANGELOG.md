# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release on its own, for Minecraft 26.2 on NeoForge and Fabric.

- The parabuzzy and the Bob-omb, from Grim Pack's Grim World (1.12), on their own.
- Parabuzzies and Bob-ombs still sit on your head, though nothing may ride a player any more. They are pinned there instead, and sneaking on the ground puts them down.
- Requires Assorted Lib 4.3.0.

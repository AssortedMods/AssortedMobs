# Assorted Treasure Mob

Now and then a chest walks off on its own inside a structure, carrying that structure's loot. A wild
one keeps away from players, but gold nuggets lure it in and may tame it; a tame one follows you and
opens as a chest. From the Grim World part of the old [Grim Pack](https://github.com/grim3212/grim-pack).

Also in [Assorted Mobs](../mobs), which is every one of these creatures in one download. Their
creative tab and manual section are shared, so they look the same either way.

Worlds made with Assorted Mobs 1.x load in this mod unchanged: its creatures, items and sounds used
to be `assortedmobs:<name>` and are aliased to their new ids, and a player's recipe book and
advancement progress carry over to the moved recipes and advancements.

Requires [Assorted Lib](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedLib). One of the mods built from
[AssortedMobs](https://github.com/AssortedMods/AssortedMobs); see its README for building and
reporting issues.
